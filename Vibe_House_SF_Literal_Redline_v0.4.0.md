# Vibe House SF — Literal Content Redline

**Editorial direction:** Present Vibe House as a house where unusually capable people gather for distinctive events, generous exchange, and conversations that change what is possible next. It is **not** a coworking space, a status club, or a networking venue. The event is the invitation; the quality of the room and what happens after are the reason people return.

> This is a proposed copy document only. It does not change the public website yet.

| Priority | Page / URL | Main change |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/` | Replace the work-first positioning with a human, event-led reason for the house to exist. |
| 2 | `/why` | Replace the AI-tool-race narrative with a case for a more generous, more generative post-exit community. |
| 3 | `/events-series` | Make the house and its atmosphere the connective tissue between every event format. |
| 4 | `/biological-founder` | Reframe health and environment as intentional hospitality, not promises of optimization. |
| 5 | `/the-founders-pharmacy` | Position the collection as the rituals and materials of hosting rather than a catalogue of health claims. |
| 6 | `/brand` | Give the brand a clear voice that avoids prestige signaling and generic startup language. |

## One-line positioning options

If a shorter homepage definition is preferred, these are the strongest options from the proposed direction:

1. **A San Francisco house for post-exit founders, exceptional gatherings, and the conversations that make the next chapter possible.**
2. **A house for founders who have already built something real—and still want to be surprised by what happens when the right people gather.**
3. **Events, dinners, and conversations for people who have already climbed the first mountain and are deciding what comes next.**

**Recommended:** Option 1. It states the audience, location, event-led format, and deeper value without sounding exclusive or over-explaining the house.
